# Yadegaar (یادگار) — Project Description

## Overview

**Yadegaar** is an interactive Persian-learning app for children and teenagers aged 3–18. It combines language learning with **stories, discovery, culture, and a motivating progression system**.

The goal is to help learners gradually build a practical Persian vocabulary while also discovering Iranian culture, literature, and interesting facts.

---

## 1. Age Groups

### Yadegaar Kids — Ages 3–10

The primary focus is:

- Learning approximately **3,000 common Persian words**
- Becoming familiar with Persian letters and simple word structures
- Short, interactive lessons
- Very short and engaging stories
- Learning through images, audio, and play
- Building motivation to continue learning

The goal is not for children to immediately read or write Persian fluently. Instead, the experience should gradually build their **Persian vocabulary and familiarity with the language**.

### Yadegaar Teens — Ages 11–18

The teen experience includes the foundations of the Kids experience, while gradually introducing more advanced content:

- Learning the 3,000 common Persian words
- Longer stories
- Persian literature
- Poetry and literary texts
- High-quality translations
- Deeper exploration of Iranian culture and history

The experience gradually moves from basic vocabulary toward **reading and understanding Persian literature**.

---

## 2. Core Learning System — Discover & Explore

A central feature of Yadegaar is a **Discovery & Exploration** system.

Users do not simply wait for the app to tell them what to learn. They can actively choose what they want to discover.

There are two main paths.

### Path 1 — Teach Me

The user enters a word in their own language.

For example:

> `tree`

Yadegaar creates a learning path that leads the learner to the Persian word:

> **درخت**

The lesson may include:

- Relevant Persian letters
- Pronunciation
- Images
- Examples
- Exercises
- Repetition
- The final Persian word

The learner starts with something they already understand and uses it as a bridge into Persian.

Completing the learning path can reward the user with, for example, **50 XP**.

### Path 2 — Explore

The user enters a Persian word they already know.

For example:

> `درخت`

Yadegaar can use that word as a starting point for discovering new content, such as:

- A higher-level lesson
- Related vocabulary
- A new story
- A new topic
- Related cultural content

This path can also reward the learner with **50 XP**.

---

## 3. Learning as the Key to Unlocking Content

A major part of Yadegaar is the concept of **Unlocking**.

Not all exciting content is immediately available.

Instead, users can see attractive locked content such as:

- Stories
- Audio stories
- Cultural content
- New stages
- Special content

For example:

> 🔒 Story: “...”

The locked story can show an attractive image, title, and possibly a short preview or demo.

The learner knows that something interesting is waiting for them, but they must first complete some Persian learning.

For example:

**Learn 5 Persian words → Unlock the first short story**

This turns language learning itself into a **path toward something exciting**.

---

## 4. Home

The Home screen is the main center of the learner's experience.

At the top, the app presents a daily recommendation.

For example:

> **Today's Lesson**  
> “م” like “مادر”

The daily lesson should be short, simple, and easy to complete.

Below it, the user can see stories that are:

- Unlocked
- In progress
- Still locked

Locked stories should still be presented with an **attractive image, title, and, when possible, a short preview** to create curiosity.

The Home screen should answer two questions:

> **What should I learn today?**  
> **What can I unlock next?**

---

## 5. ➕ — Teach Me

The ➕ screen is the starting point for active, user-driven learning.

The user interacts with Yadegaar primarily in their own language.

For example:

> **Teach me**

Then they enter:

> `tree`

The app creates a Persian learning path:

**tree → درخت**

This should be one of the simplest and most engaging parts of the application. Anything the learner encounters in everyday life can become an opportunity to learn Persian.

---

## 6. 🌎 — Facts & Culture

The 🌎 section provides **facts and cultural knowledge about Iran**.

Content is presented in two languages:

**Persian + the user's native language**

The goal is not only language learning.

Users also discover topics such as:

- Iranian culture
- Iranian history
- Important cities and places
- Food
- Festivals and celebrations
- Cultural figures
- Animals and nature
- Literature
- Traditions and customs

This section should also work for children who cannot yet read Persian.

A child may only recognize:

- A few Persian letters
- A few familiar words
- Images
- Pronunciation

while reading the main fact in their own language.

They can still learn **something new about Iran**.

This makes the section a bridge between **language learning and cultural discovery**.

---

## 7. 🚹 — Profile / Me

The Profile screen is built around a simple personal concept:

> **من**

The word “Me” / “من” appears prominently at the top.

Below it, the user's statistics are presented as personal statements.

For example:

> **I know 97 words.**  
> **I have read 8 stories.**  
> **I have learned 3 facts.**

The page should create a sense of ownership and progress:

> **This is what I have learned so far.**

---

## 8. Progress — The Journey to 3,000 Words

The main learning goal is to reach **3,000 Persian words**.

Instead of showing only a large and distant goal, the journey can be divided into smaller milestones.

For example:

**0 → 100 → 200 → 300 → ... → 3000**

Every 100 words can represent a **Level Up**.

For example:

> 🎉 Level 3  
> You know 300 Persian words!

This turns the large goal of 3,000 words into smaller, achievable milestones.

---

## 9. XP & Motivation

Yadegaar uses a simple XP system to encourage continuous learning.

Users can earn XP through activities such as:

| Activity | Example Reward |
|---|---:|
| Complete a new learning path | +50 XP |
| Discover a Persian word | +50 XP |
| Complete a lesson | XP |
| Read a story | XP |
| Learn a fact | XP |

XP works alongside word count, stories, and facts to create a continuous sense of progress.

---

## 10. Product Experience Philosophy

Yadegaar should not feel like a **digital textbook**.

The desired experience is closer to:

**Discover → Learn → Unlock → Explore → Learn More**

The learner sees something interesting.

For example, a story.

But the story is locked.

To unlock it, the learner needs to learn several Persian words.

After learning them, the story becomes available.

Inside the story, the learner encounters new words.

Those words become new learning opportunities.

This creates a continuous loop:

**Curiosity → Learning → Reward → Curiosity**

---

## 11. Vision

Yadegaar's vision is to make learning Persian for children and teenagers—especially those who do not live in a Persian-speaking environment—a **natural, enjoyable, and culturally meaningful experience**.

The learner is not simply learning Persian.

They are:

**Learning Persian, discovering stories, exploring Iran, and building a sense of progress along the way.**

Ultimately, the goal is to transform the journey from:

> **“I don't know Persian.”**

into:

> **“I know 3,000 Persian words and I can read Persian stories.”**

into an engaging, measurable, and rewarding journey.
